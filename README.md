tap.near
A community trading desk for NEAR. Whitepaper v0.1 — October 2026
This note describes the product, the public treasury, the optional token, and the limits of what the project does
and does not promise. It is not an offer of securities, not a promise of profit, and not financial advice.
1. What it is
tap.near is a one-tap trading desk for the NEAR network: a website and a Telegram bot. The first ship is market
buy and sell through Rhea. Later stages add stop-loss, take-profit, OCO, DCA ladders and multi-wallet actions.
The point is to cut the approval and tab-switching that frequent NEAR traders still do by hand.
Site: https://tapnear.online — X: https://x.com/tapnearbot — Telegram: https://t.me/tapnear_bot
2. Wallets
Three named accounts. They are not mixed.
taptreasury.near — public treasury. Contributions land here. It does not trade. The site reads this balance for the
raise bar.
taphot.near — operations. Hosting, RPC and development are paid from here, with a public memo on each
transfer.
tapbot.near — trading hot wallet for the bot. Trader deposits for the product go here. Treasury funds do not.
3. Funding
The raise is staged so a small desk can ship without asking for a large lump sum: Stage 1 $4,000 (market
buy/sell, wallet factory, Rhea routing), Stage 2 $8,000 total (stop-loss, take-profit, OCO), Stage 3 $12,000 total
(DCA, multi-wallet, trailing). Hard cap $15,000.
A contribution is a donation to build the desk. It is not a token sale and it is not a share. If a token is launched
later, an airdrop to the contributor snapshot may be made, with vesting. No APY is paid on deposits.
Contributor perks, if the product ships: fee holidays, fee discounts, a higher referral cut, and a public backer slot
on the site. Details live on tapnear.online and can change before Stage 1 closes.
4. Token
A ticker may be launched on a NEAR launchpad (NearBased, JustHoot, Nearly or similar) so the pool trades on
Rhea. Liquidity on those launchers is locked. Buying the token is not a treasury deposit and does not fill the
raise bar. Intended utility, once the bot is live: lower trading fees, a referral boost, and a buyback funded by a
slice of desk fees. Until the bot ships, the token is a community mark, not a working discount.
5. How the desk is paid
Planned product fee: 0.7% of notional, of which about 35% returns through cashback and referrals. Founder
draw, after Stage 1 is live: $400 a month for three months, paid from ops, not by trading the treasury. The
treasury does not market-make and does not trade memes.
6. Risks and responsibility
Software, wallets and websites fail. NEAR, Rhea, the launcher, the host and the domain are third parties. The
project does not custody funds beyond what a person chooses to send to the published accounts, and it does not
guarantee execution, uptime or price.
If the website is hacked, defaced, taken down by a DDoS, or the domain or host is compromised, tap.near and
its operator are not responsible for funds lost, trades that fail, or decisions made from a fake page. Always
check the address on NearBlocks before sending: taptreasury.near. A cloned site is not the treasury.
If a bug, outage or attack has to be fixed and the treasury cannot cover it, the project will ask the community for
a new public raise. That raise is voluntary. It is not an obligation of past contributors, and it is not a refund
mechanism.
Nothing here limits rights that the law does not allow to be waived. This is a project policy, not a court
judgment.
